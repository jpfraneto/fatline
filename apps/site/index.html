FATLINE

A Farcaster client · a USDC launchpad

More copies of Farcaster.

Fatline is a Farcaster client that reads from Snapchain or Hypersnap, the two networks that carry Farcaster, through a node you choose and can see. And a launchpad on Base: every coin is paired with USDC, and its trading fees pay a server that keeps a full copy of the network.

1 · Pick the network you read

Two networks. You choose. It always says which.

Snapchain is run by Farcaster. Hypersnap is a community fork that keeps full history. Fatline treats them as equals: switch at the top of this page, or in your client's Settings. Below is what each network looks like right now, read live.

2 · The launchpad pays for the copies

Fees in USDC keep a node alive.

  1. A coinLaunched on Clanker, paired with USDC. One per Fatliner, at most 777 ever. Its ticker is the Fatliner's surname; nobody picks it.
  2. Its fees100% of the coin's creator fees go to its own vault, paid in USDC. The contract refuses a launch that sends them anywhere else.
  3. The rentAnyone can trigger payCompute: the vault pays the server's keeper, never more than the agreed rate for the period.
  4. A copyThat server runs a full Snapchain or Hypersnap node. If the vault runs dry, or its holder takes the money out, the node goes dark, and it shows.

On Base (chain 8453): Fatliners + launchpad 0xb54C…3016 · USDC 0x8335…2913 · every address. Today: the contract is live, eggs are open, $FATLINE (the first coin) is not launched yet.

3 · Run a copy yourself

Your node and your client, in three commands.

# install the fatline command; it then walks you through the three below
curl -fsSL https://fatline.ink/install.sh | sh

fatline compute          # rent a server for the node (USDC, by the day)
fatline node snapchain   # a full node on it, no API keys  (or: node hypersnap)
fatline client           # your client, reading from your node
