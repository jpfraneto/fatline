FATLINE

Farcaster, on your own machine

Run your own Farcaster.

Fatline puts a full copy of Farcaster on a server you control, with a client that reads only from it. Nobody in between can filter your feed or switch you off.

Three commands, no API keys

# install the fatline command; it then offers to walk you through the three below
curl -fsSL https://fatline.ink/install.sh | sh

fatline compute          # (0) rent a machine that can hold a node
fatline node snapchain   # (1) your own read node on it  (or: node hypersnap)
fatline client           # (2) your client, reading only from your node

The tutorial explains each step and the protocol behind it → English · Español · 中文

Already run a Snapchain or Hypersnap node? One line on its server: curl -fsSL https://fatline.ink/join.sh | sudo bash

Your node

All of Farcaster (Snapchain or Hypersnap, your pick) streams into a server you rent. From about $2.60 a day.

Your client

Read and cast through your own node, with your own account. You can start before it finishes syncing.

Your Fatliner

Every hour, the network checks your node. Keep it live for three days and it can earn you one of 777 Fatliners, each with its own coin.
