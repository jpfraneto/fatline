Coins that keep Farcaster running.

Farcaster is a protocol, not a company's server: anyone can keep a full copy of every cast and every follow, and build their own window onto it. Each coin here funds one of those copies and the independent client built on it. Its trading fees, in USDC, pay the rent. Pick any node below and read Farcaster through it.

How a coin pays for a node

  1. A copySomeone runs a full Snapchain or Hypersnap node: all of Farcaster, on their own server, with their own client. Three warm days and its egg hatches into a Fatliner (777 ever).
  2. A coinThe Fatliner's coin launches on Clanker, paired with USDC. Its ticker is the Fatliner's surname.
  3. Its fees100% of the coin's fees go to its own vault, in USDC.
  4. The rentThe vault pays the node's bills, never more than the agreed rate. Dry vault, dark node: it shows here.
