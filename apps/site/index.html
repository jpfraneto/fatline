FATLINE

A Farcaster node with a life of its own.

A Fatline is a server that keeps a full copy of Farcaster (Snapchain, Hypersnap, or both), serves its own client, and is built to pay its own rent in USDC, from its own wallet. These are the ones alive right now.

About 11 USDC a day for one node, approved in your own wallet. No card, no account. Or your own server, for less.
1 · RentA server by the day, in USDC on Base (x402). You approve it in your own wallet: phone or browser. Or bring your own server.
2 · SyncThe node streams a snapshot of the network (~400 GB) and catches up: a few hours.
3 · ReadYour client reads from your own node, with no API in between. Rent is paid a day at a time; unpaid, it stops.

How it pays its own rent: a Fatline can launch its own coin, paired with USDC. Its trading fees go to its vault, and the vault pays the server, a day at a time. Not live yet: the coins launch once the contracts are on Base.
