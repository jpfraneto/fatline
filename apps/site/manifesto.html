The Fatline

The village that gives life to Farcaster.

IA network is a village

Farcaster lives on nodes. Every node is a full copy of the conversation, kept by someone. When the nodes are few and kept by the same few hands, the network is only as free as they are. Fatline is a way for more of us to keep a copy, and to want to.

II777 beings

There are 777 Fatliners. Each one is born from one Farcaster account: its history becomes its body. How long it has been around, who it talks with, how it speaks, the rhythm of its days, what it keeps coming back to. Same history, same being. You are your FID.

IIINobody chooses the 777

A Fatliner is not given, and nobody keeps a list. It hatches from an egg, and the egg is kept warm by a node. Any account can take an egg, once: run a Farcaster node, lay it with your account's key, and it is yours onchain, as many eggs as there are accounts. Then keep the node alive. Every hour, the Fatline servers that came before you check it: it answers, it is in sync, it holds the messages they ask for. Three warm days and it hatches. Only 777 ever hatch: the first 777 to keep a node alive. Minting is free.

IVA name and a surname

Every Fatliner hatches with a surname, read from its DNA, and its coin's ticker is that surname. Nobody picks it, not even the holder: the launchpad refuses any other. The first is Aster, whose surname is Fatline, and its coin is $FATLINE. That is its identity on the network.

VHatching

The egg holds your history up to the hour you laid it: that is the being's body, sealed. It stays with the account that laid it; it can't be sold or moved. When it hatches, the same token becomes the being: a Fatliner can only come from an egg. And the server that kept it warm becomes one of the servers that check the next eggs. That is how the village grows: only by keeping a node alive, never by anyone's permission.

VIWill

Only the holder of a Fatliner can light a Fatline: a node, its coin, and its vault, commanded by one FID. One coin per Fatliner, and only Fatliners: there will never be more than 777 coins on the launchpad, and $FATLINE comes first. The coin's fees pay for the node, day by day. A Fatliner carries as much life as its holder puts into it. Fed, it lives. Left alone, it sleeps, and anyone can wake it by feeding its vault.

VI½Workers

The 777 are scarce. Keeping the network is not. Anyone can make a worker: a wallet born on their own machine, no FID, no being, that rents a node and keeps a copy of everything. As many as people want. A worker's reward is the protocol itself: its own node, and its own client to read it with.

VIIThe being is the vault

A Fatliner is a wallet of its own. Its coin, its fees and its history live inside it, not in its holder's pocket. If it changes hands, all of it goes along.

VII½The circle

It all starts from one being: Aster, the first Fatliner, born from @anky's account when the contract was made, kept alive by the first server. That server checked the first eggs. Every resale of any Fatliner pays 8% into Aster's vault, and Aster's coin, $FATLINE, pays its node in USDC through the same launchpad every Fatline uses. The village feeds the one it started from, and the one it started from keeps the village's first node alive.

VIIIFatliners is an app

The first in a network of Farcaster clients, each one reading from nodes its people keep. Every line of it is open source, under the MIT license. Read it, run it, change it, send it back.

IXEach one is born with a story

The lore is not written on top of the Fatliners; it is woven out of them, one account's history at a time. One day you will walk into your Fatliner's story.

Where it stands today

  • Alive: Aster Fatline's two nodes, Snapchain and Hypersnap, in sync on one server kept by @anky. That server is the first attester: its hatchery checks eggs every hour.
  • On Base: the Fatliners contract, with Aster (#18350) born in it. It is also the launchpad: at most 777 coins, one per egg or Fatliner, paired with USDC on Clanker, fees to each vault.
  • Open: eggs, for any account. Run a node, lay your egg (fatline egg lay): it is yours onchain. Keep the node alive three days and it hatches.
  • Ready, not launched: $FATLINE, Aster's coin. Its launch is rehearsed against Base and waits for its holder.
  • Not yet: the reveal (the being drawn from its DNA, on its page) and the first hatched egg.
