Log in with Fatline

Any app can know which Farcaster account its visitor is, from one signature in their wallet. No seed phrase, no gas, nothing moves. No company in the middle either: a Fatline node checks it against the Farcaster network, and anyone can check it again with another node.

How it works

  1. You say which account is yours: a Farcaster name or an FID. Inside Farcaster, the app already knows.
  2. Your wallet signs a short message: this site, this account, a code, the time. It's the standard "Sign in with Ethereum" message.
  3. A Fatline node checks two things against the network: that your wallet made the signature, and that the wallet is this account's. That means its owner (custody) address, or an address the account verified on Farcaster.

Logging in proves who you are. It doesn't let the app post as you. Posting needs a key that you approve in the Farcaster app, and that doesn't need a recovery phrase either.

Add it to your app

<script src="https://fatline.ink/login.js"></script>
<div id="login"></div>
<script>
  FatlineLogin.button(document.getElementById('login'), {
    onLogin: (who) => console.log(who.fid, who.address, who.how),
  });
</script>

Options: node (any Fatline node; default https://fatline.fatline.ink), provider (a wallet; inside a Farcaster mini app, sdk.wallet.ethProvider), fid, nonce, network (farcaster or hypersnap).

Check it on your server

POST https://fatline.fatline.ink/api/auth/verify
{ "message": "…", "signature": "0x…", "domain": "your.app", "nonce": "your nonce" }

→ { "fid": 16098, "address": "0x…", "how": "verified", "expiresAt": "…" }

Every Fatline node answers this, so you can ask yours, ours, or several. A login lasts ten minutes by default, and a day at most. It only works for the domain and nonce it names.
