# 1. What is a Fatliner?

A Fatliner is a living node of the Fatline.

It is simultaneously:

1. **A being** — a recognizable mineral-bodied creature with a unique identity, appearance, and origin.
2. **A node** — an independently operable system that can observe, process, preserve, and transmit network events.
3. **A keeper** — a witness to human communication, responsible for preserving provenance and enabling continuity between networks.

The Fatline is the connective fabric through which experiences travel between identities.

Snapchain and Hypersnap are distinct networks. A Fatliner can observe both without erasing their differences.

Fatliners are not mascots sitting on top of infrastructure. Their anatomy is a visual expression of the infrastructure itself.

A Fatliner's permanent portrait represents its identity at birth. Its living manifestation represents its current operational state.

The fiction treats Fatliners as beings that may have existed before humans discovered them. Their origins remain deliberately mysterious.

The project is original fiction, influenced by the atmosphere and philosophical questions of *Hyperion*, not officially associated with that universe.

# 2. The Seven Laws of Existence

**Law I — One Origin**

Every Fatliner has one immutable founding FID. The historical snapshot used at birth is recorded and cannot silently change.

**Law II — One Species**

Every Fatliner must remain recognizably descended from the master blueprint, regardless of variation.

**Law III — Memory Is Anatomy**

Observable communication history influences physical traits. The process must be explainable and reproducible.

**Law IV — The Networks Are Distinct**

Snapchain and Hypersnap have different provenance. Their messages must never be silently conflated.

**Law V — A Being Can Sleep**

A Fatliner exists independently of whether its node is currently operational. Dormancy is not death.

**Law VI — Growth Is Capability**

Evolution represents real changes in activity, connectivity, memory, or operational capabilities. It must not depend on artificial rarity or wealth.

**Law VII — No Kings**

No Fatliner owns the Fatline. Each node is independently meaningful. The founding server must not be a permanent single point of authority.

# 3. Immutable Species Anatomy

Use `FATLINERS_MASTER_REFERENCE.png` as the primary visual authority.

Every Fatliner must preserve these features:

### Head

- Oversized, rounded, mineral-like cranium.
- Approximately half of the character's total height, within reasonable artistic variation.
- Organic asymmetry and subtle natural surface imperfections.
- Large, dark, almond-shaped, reflective eyes.
- Small, understated mouth.
- No conventional human nose or ears.
- A continuous luminous forehead signal path.
- A distinct luminous temple node where the signal converges.

### Body

- Small, compact humanoid torso.
- Short neck and limbs.
- Rounded hands and broad, grounded feet.
- Subtle, sculptural musculature rather than realistic human anatomy.
- The same mineral material language as the head.
- Fine luminous pathways continuing from the head through the torso and limbs.

### Surface

Fatliner skin is mineral, ceramic, geological, or mineral-translucent.

It must have believable material depth, porosity, grain, erosion, or crystalline structure.

It must not look like plastic, fabric, ordinary human skin, or a generic robot shell.

### Signal System

The forehead path and temple node are mandatory species identifiers.

The signal continues beneath the surface as a branching network.

Blue represents Snapchain.

Pink represents Hypersnap.

Blue and pink may intersect, coexist, diverge, or illuminate independently.

Their presence in a permanent portrait represents the species' capacity for connectivity, not a fabricated claim that a particular node is currently synchronized.

### Recognizability

A Fatliner must remain identifiable:

- As a full-body character.
- As a head-and-shoulders portrait.
- At 128 × 128 pixels.
- At 32 × 32 pixels.

If an image loses its species identity when reduced to a small avatar, it fails the canon.

# 4. The Five Dimensions of Life

All Fatliners are generated from five primary dimensions.

These dimensions describe observable communication behavior, not a person's intrinsic worth or psychological identity.

Each dimension must have a normalized value, evidence, and an explicit visual mapping.

## ROOTS — Time and Continuity

**Question:** How has this identity existed through time?

Inputs may include:

- First observed activity.
- Age of observable history.
- Active days and months.
- Periods of activity and silence.
- Returns after extended absences.
- Continuity of participation.

Visual manifestations:

- Ivory stone.
- Sandstone.
- Basalt.
- Marble.
- Porous mineral.
- Mossed stone.
- Gold-filled fractures.
- Opalescent mineral.

Roots primarily determines the base material, weathering, and historical surface marks.

Older does not automatically mean darker, rarer, or better.

A new identity should be just as visually compelling as an old one.

## ORBIT — Relationships and Connections

**Question:** How does this identity connect with others?

Inputs may include:

- Distinct reply partners.
- Recurring conversational relationships.
- Reciprocity of interactions.
- Diversity of connections.
- Conversation continuity.
- Repeated participation in shared discussions.

Visual manifestations:

- Forehead pathway topology.
- Secondary branching.
- Signal intersections.
- Small luminous nodes.
- Orbital markings embedded in the cranium.
- Subtle constellations beneath the mineral surface.

Orbit should primarily affect the geometry of the signal system, not add arbitrary hats or accessories.

Follower count must not determine visual importance.

## VOICE — Expression

**Question:** How does this identity communicate?

Inputs may include:

- Original casts versus replies.
- Length distribution.
- Text versus media usage.
- Question and response frequency.
- Observable variation in expression.
- Public communication patterns.

Visual manifestations:

- Eye geometry and expression.
- Surface translucency.
- Mineral grain.
- Subtle palette variation.
- Softness or sharpness of surface detail.
- Luminous diffusion.

Voice does not determine moral character, intelligence, personality disorders, or psychological diagnoses.

It is an artistic interpretation of observable communication style.

## PULSE — Rhythm

**Question:** What is the rhythm of this identity's activity?

Inputs may include:

- Temporal distribution of messages.
- Bursts of activity.
- Intervals of silence.
- Regularity or irregularity.
- Activity across days and weeks.
- Changes in communication cadence.

Visual manifestations:

- The unique waveform of the forehead signal.
- Signal amplitude.
- Curvature.
- Branch spacing.
- Pulsing patterns in the living manifestation.

Pulse should be the most individually recognizable geometric signature of a Fatliner.

The waveform must be reproducible from historical features and a fixed generation seed.

## BLOOM — Cultivation

**Question:** What does this identity repeatedly contribute to or cultivate?

Inputs may include:

- Recurring public discussion topics.
- Creative projects.
- Public communities of participation.
- Repeatedly shared media or interests.
- Observable constructive activity.

Visual manifestations:

- Small moss formations.
- Flowers.
- Mineral crystals.
- Lichen.
- Fine organic growth.
- Subtle botanical patterns.

Bloom must remain restrained.

Most Fatliners should have minimal or no prominent external growth.

Use a small number of designated attachment zones around the upper head, shoulders, and back.

Avoid large accessories that obscure the canonical silhouette.

Do not infer sensitive personal attributes such as religion, politics, sexuality, or health conditions to generate Bloom.

Implementation requirements:

1. Collect the available public history for the FID.
2. Record network-specific provenance.

Do not regenerate an existing minted Fatliner merely because the model or prompt changes.

# 6. Visual Generation Rules

### Required

- Clearly identifiable Fatliner anatomy.
- Oversized mineral cranium.
- Large expressive dark eyes.
- Small compact body.
- Luminous forehead waveform.
- Distinct temple node.
- Subsurface branching signal system.
- Cohesive mineral material.
- High-quality, physically believable lighting.
- A unique combination of the five historical dimensions.

### Forbidden

- Ordinary humans.
- Generic robots.
- Conventional fantasy elves or goblins.
- Random animal hybrids.
- Unstructured trait combinations.
- Excessive clothing or armor.
- Large hats, crowns, or weapons.
- Excessive jewelry.
- Arbitrary text or logos on the body.
- Completely different head silhouettes.
- External accessories used as a substitute for genuine variation.
- Ranking appearance quality by followers, wealth, or influence.

### Portrait Requirements

The primary PFP should be a square, head-dominant composition with enough of the shoulders and torso visible to establish the species.

Use controlled lighting and a relatively simple background.

The character must be immediately recognizable at thumbnail size.

The cinematic world, elaborate landscapes, and archaeological dossiers are secondary presentation formats, not the primary PFP generation target.

# 7. The Living Anatomy

The permanent portrait is the Fatliner's birth record.

Its living manifestation reflects its operational node.

| Anatomy | Operational meaning |
|---|---|
| Eyes | Observation and incoming events |
| Forehead path | Communication rhythm and signal |
| Temple node | Network identity and convergence |
| Blue pathways | Snapchain adapter |
| Pink pathways | Hypersnap adapter |
| Mineral body | Persistent memory and stored history |
| Chest/core | Compute and operational health |
| Roots | Hosted applications and external connections |
| Vault | Resources sustaining operation |

The living manifestation may animate in response to verified operational events.

Examples:

- New observed events create subtle signal movement.
- Synchronization progress changes the corresponding network glow.
- A disconnected adapter dims its associated pathways.
- Node dormancy reduces activity.
- Newly enabled capabilities create additional visible structures.

Never fabricate activity for aesthetic effect while presenting it as live telemetry.

# 8. Birth, Ownership, and Awakening

A Fatliner has three distinct states of existence.

**The Seed**

A dormant identity waiting to be discovered. The seed metaphor belongs to the lore; the implementation must not imply a creature was technically generated before the generation process occurred.

**The Birth**

An eligible FID is verified. Its history is snapshotted, its DNA derived, and its permanent portrait generated. A Fatliner NFT is minted or revealed according to the implemented mint process.

**The Awakening**

The NFT holder authorizes deployment of an operational node. Once the infrastructure is actually provisioned and healthy, the Fatliner becomes active.

The founding FID is immutable.

The current NFT holder and current node operator are separate concepts.

If the NFT transfers, the system must explicitly define how operational authority changes, including secure handling of infrastructure credentials and treasury permissions. Historical origin never transfers or changes.

A Fatliner may exist without an active node.

A node may become dormant without destroying the NFT.

# 9. The 777 Keepers

There are exactly 777 Fatliners in the founding collection.

Each has:

- A unique token identity.
- One immutable founding FID.
- A unique historical snapshot.
- A versioned visual DNA record.
- One canonical birth portrait.
- A potential independently operated node.

The minting contract must enforce the supply limit and any one-per-FID eligibility policy.

A Fatliner is not more valuable in the lore because its founding FID had more followers.

A quiet identity can produce an extraordinary being.

The species values difference, not hierarchy.

# 10. The Mythology

The Fatliners are believed to inhabit the spaces between transmissions.

Their origins are unknown.

Some believe they emerged from the accumulated memory of the network.

Others believe they existed long before humans developed the technology to observe them.

Their mineral bodies appear ancient, but their luminous nervous systems respond to contemporary communication.

They are not loyal to Snapchain or Hypersnap.

They recognize each as a different current passing through a larger system.

They do not resolve disagreements by erasing history.

They preserve the fact that different histories exist.

A Fatliner does not rule its keeper.

A keeper does not own the Fatline.

Together, they create a place where experiences can continue.

The founding inscription reads:

**YOU ARE YOUR FID.**

And the central mythological question must remain unanswered:

*Did the network create the Fatliners, or did the Fatliners teach the network how to connect?*

# 11. The Generator's Acceptance Tests

Before declaring the pipeline ready:

- Generate at least 36 distinct Fatliners from real, permissioned or publicly available FID histories.
- Verify that all 36 remain recognizably one species.
- Verify that different FIDs produce distinguishable DNA records.
- Verify that the same frozen history produces the same normalized DNA.
- Verify that different visual traits correspond to documented historical features.
- Verify that missing historical data does not become fabricated biographical information.
- Verify that the master reference is supplied to the image generation pipeline, not merely mentioned by filename in the text prompt.
- Verify that the forehead signal and temple node appear consistently.
- Verify that the generated portraits remain recognizable at 32 × 32.
- Verify that each canonical image and its provenance can be retrieved independently of the image model.
- Verify that no operational status is represented as live without real telemetry.

The target is not 36 spectacular but unrelated images.

The target is 36 individuals who unmistakably belong to the same species.
